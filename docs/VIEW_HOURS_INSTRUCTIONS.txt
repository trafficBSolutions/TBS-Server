          TRAFFIC & BARRIER SOLUTIONS, LLC
          HOW TO VIEW YOUR WEEKLY HOURS
================================================================================

STEP 1:  Go to the Employee Dashboard
        ─────────────────────────────────
        • Open the website: www.trafficbarriersolutions.com
        • Click "Employee Login"
        • Log in with the employee email and password


STEP 2:  Scroll down to the Time Clock section
        ─────────────────────────────────
        • You will see "⏰ Time Clock" on your dashboard
        • Below the clock in/out area, there is a section that says
          "Enter PIN to view hours"


STEP 3:  Enter your PIN
        ─────────────────────────────────
        • Type your 4-digit PIN in the box
        • Click the "📅 View My Weekly Hours" button


STEP 4:  View your hours
        ─────────────────────────────────
        • Your hours for the current week (Saturday – Friday) will appear
        • You will see:
            - Your name
            - Each day of the week
            - What time you clocked in and out each day
            - Total hours for each day
            - Your TOTAL hours for the week


================================================================================
                         IMPORTANT REMINDERS
================================================================================

  • Your PIN is private. Do NOT share it with anyone.

  • If you forgot your PIN, call Carson: (706) 581-4465

  • The week runs Saturday through Friday.

  • If you see "Still In" next to a day, that means you are currently
    clocked in and haven't clocked out yet.

  • You can view your hours from ANY device (phone, computer, tablet).
    You do NOT need to be at the shop.

  • Clocking in/out is ONLY available on the tablet at the shop
    (must be on shop WiFi), but VIEWING hours works anywhere.
